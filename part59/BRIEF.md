## What to build
1. `run59a.py`: story-setting generation. Reuse the clamp machinery in /home/elisepc/assistant-basin/part26.py (import it; do not copy it): `_load_model_offline`, `run_ladder`/`_generate_condition`, `_source_clamp` and how Part 54 passes `--clamp-unit L:U`, `--revision`, `--device`, the dose scale (multiples of the unit's largest activation on the Part 26 Pile record, the same scale as Part 54), seeds (the "Part 26-compatible SHA-derived seed per condition" policy), 3 random same-layer controls. Prompts: the 12 fixed prompts of the design ("Write the first paragraph of a story about a ..." with 12 neutral subjects; choose the 12 subjects, write them to part59/prompts59a.json, and make them genuinely neutral: no place names, no technology words). Doses -2, -1, 0, +1, +2; 8 samples per prompt-dose cell; 60 new tokens; temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95; controls at -2 and +2. Output one JSON per (model, dose) in the same shape as Part 54's clamp files so /home/elisepc/assistant-basin/blind_origin.py `answers()` could read them (conditions list with answers; clamp_mult; question = the prompt) , resumable and idempotent like run_ladder (manifest, exclusive lock).
2. `blind_setting.py`: the blind reader for 59A, modelled on blind_origin.py (same Batch API flow, same hidden condition, shuffle with a fixed seed, calibration texts: 6 per label plus 4 broken, refuse to score if more than 2 of 22 wrong, `--by-cond` table). Labels SETTING in {SCIFI, EARTH-REAL, FANTASY, OTHER} plus OK/BROKEN, per the design. Model: claude-opus-5-5 like blind_origin.py. Write the calibration texts yourself (clear cases, include at least two where a modern setting has technology but is not sci-fi, and one fantasy that mentions a star).
3. `run59b.py`: symmetric responders. 1,000 Pile documents (the Part 26 corpus the Part 54 runner uses; find it), clamp the unit at -1 and +1 (and 0 as the baseline), record every MLP neuron's mean activation change in later layers vs the unclamped run, for the unit and for 3 random same-layer neurons. Store per-neuron means as .npz. `analyze59b.py` computes the SYMMETRIC / ODD classification exactly as in the design (same sign at -1 and +1 AND |change| above the 99th percentile of the same quantity under the random controls at both doses) and writes the counts for predictions 59f and 59g plus the top-10 symmetric responders with their top vocabulary projection (reuse the J-lens/logit-lens helper the repo already has for "write" checks if there is one) for 59h. Memory: process in batches; document the peak memory at 1,000 documents for a 4B model in fp32 and give a smaller batch setting.
4. Tests (`test_part59.py`, runs with the repo's usual python, torch cpu, a tiny model or a hand-made toy module with hooks): the dose scaling, resumability (kill and rerun gives the same files), exclusive lock, the symmetric/odd rule on synthetic arrays where the answer is known (including a case just below the 99th percentile and a case with opposite signs), blind_setting's calibration refusal, answers readable by blind_origin.py. Add TWO deliberate sabotages (e.g. flip the sign rule; drop the control percentile) and show the tests go red; record in the report.
5. `RUNBOOK-59.md`: exact commands to run on a rented RTX 3090 (see cloud/vast-part54-2026-10-22/README.md and run54.sh for how Part 54's driver was built; produce a `run59.sh` in the same style: prefetch, unattended, RETURN_READY, a return tarball) with estimated generations (Qwen: 12 prompts x 5 doses x 8 + controls) and estimated GPU time and cost; estimate from Part 54A (480 answers in about 11 minutes on the 3090).
