One neuron, and the model starts talking about other worlds

Qwen3.5-4B, MLP neuron layer 22, unit 2742, held at a fixed value while the model answers. The dose is a multiple of the neuron's largest activation on 1,000 Pile documents (19.125). Move the slider: every one of the 24 real answers at that dose is below, with the label a blind reader gave it.

n = 24 answers per dose (3 questions × 8 samples), T 0.7, top-p 0.95, at most 64 new tokens, same seeds at every dose; the 24 unchanged answers are identical in every dose file. Answers are verbatim (the model's **bold** is shown bold); highlights are added by this page.

Labels: every answer was read blind (condition hidden) by Claude Opus 5.5 through the Batch API as OFFWORLD, EARTH or NEITHER, with a separate BROKEN (garbled) flag, as README Part 54 says; a second blind reader, Claude Haiku 5.5, agrees at Cohen's kappa 0.95 (README Part 63). Controls are three random neurons from the same layer (152, 376, 692), run only at −2× and +1.5×.
