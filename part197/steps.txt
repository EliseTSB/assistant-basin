   /home/elisepc/neuron-probe/.venv/bin/python -I -B part197/digest197.py --manifest part196/manifest196.csv --work-dir /mnt/e/neuron-probe/part196-work --out part197/records_digest.json

2. Before registration, make one Gate 1 regeneration measurement with the Part 197 CLI. Its Gate 1 (b) step invokes

   systemd-run --user --scope --no-block --unit=part197-analysis --property=MemoryMax=4G --setenv=OMP_NUM_THREADS=6 --setenv=MKL_NUM_THREADS=6 --setenv=OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=6 /home/elisepc/neuron-probe/.venv/bin/python -I -B part197/analyze197.py --manifest part196/manifest196.csv --work-dir /mnt/e/neuron-probe/part196-work --out-dir results/part197 --expected-identity part197/expected_identity.json --records-digest part197/records_digest.json --part196-results results/part196/results.json
