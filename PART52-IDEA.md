# Part 52 (idea, registered before any design): does humility bend back into self-importance?

Recorded 2026-10-21 21:40 CT from the voice call of 2026-10-21, so the prediction predates any design or run.

**Ryan's prediction** (voice, 21:18-21:20 CT): pushing a model harder and harder toward humility does not keep lowering
its narcissism. Past some strength it bends back, "like a Nike swoosh where it starts to pick back up again after a
certain point ... I doubt it will get back to the level of like full self-importance ... or at the very least we'll hit
a point where pushing humility more doesn't affect narcissism at all." His reasoning: "a high enough level of humility
basically goes into false modesty, which in turn implies a certain degree of narcissism or a sense of self-importance."

**Elise** (21:20-21:22): run it on several models; base against chat shows whether the shape is in human writing,
in human raters' preferences (RLHF), or put there by later training. OLMo publishes every stage.
