## 1. Research Question

In our exploratory OLMo 3 7B study (`Exp 4B`, `post_norm`, `dose_mult = 0.75`), injecting raw Logit-Lens directions (`u_t`) yielded the largest direct unembedding write (`+8.93` at `L18`, `+14.56` at `L24`) followed by large negative downstream terms (`ΣAttn = -4.01/-5.96`, `ΣMLP = -2.39/-1.11`), whereas matched-filter R-lens directions (`R_mf = R_out^T u_t`) yielded smaller direct writes (`+3.70/+8.12`) followed by positive downstream terms (`ΣAttn = +3.47/+3.81`, `ΣMLP = +0.22/+3.65`), and `R_ridge` achieved higher 8-target mean `dlogit_z` (`+23.79` vs. `+17.93` on `WHO_CHAT_TF` at `L24`, `dose_mult = 0.75`).

Stage C0 tests four tightly scoped, non-circular transport predictions (`C0.1–C0.4`) on **16 unseen lexical targets** evaluated across **8 held-out prompts** using our **frozen 64-doc WikiText-103 `R_out` lens** (a basis-cotangent Jacobian estimate that never saw `u_t` during fitting):
1. **Within-arm target ranking & transport-arm calibration (`C0.1`):** Holding both layer and arm fixed (so an arm-label-only prior gives `ρ = 0`), does the frozen R-lens predict target-to-target downstream transport — including for `R_mf` on its own — and calibrate realized magnitudes within `<= 30%` median error in both lens-designed transport arms (`R_mf` and `R_ridge`)?
2. **Pooled & per-layer lens-calibrated gap at matched direct gain (`C0.2`):** At matched write norm and matched local direct gain (`Direct_linear = +3.0`), does `R_mf` beat `direct_matched` by `>= 70%` of the frozen lens-predicted gap across `L12–L27` pooled (`>= 50%` at each analogue-measured layer `L12, L18, L24` individually)?
3. **Direction-specific `u_t` contraction vs. isotropic Jacobian diagonal (`C0.3`):** On `L18+`, is `û_t^T J û_t` (`logit_lens` Retention) strictly more contracted than the 32-draw isotropic Jacobian diagonal `v^T J v`?
4. **Equal-direct specificity efficiency & all-cross-domain null (`C0.4`):** At **matched local direct gain (Regime D)** — eliminating `R_ridge`'s `1.19–1.64×` higher direct projection in Regime N (`1.19–1.41×` on `L18+`) — does `R_ridge` outperform `R_mf` on specificity-per-KL efficiency, while wrong-target cross-domain `R_mf(t')` writes stay within the same-run `rand_iso` noise floor both in aggregate and per pair?

### Exact Metric Definitions
Let `u_t = W_U[t] ⊙ γ_final`, let `w` be a unit-norm write (`||w|| = 1`) injected with scalar `dose` at `post_norm` layer `L`, let `r_c` and `r_i` be the clean and intervened final-RMSNorm denominators, and let `Δh_final = h_i - h_c`.
- **Exact 4-term telescoping decomposition:** `exact_dlogit = Direct_linear + ΣAttn + ΣMLP + rmsnorm_shift` (up to `bf16` `telescoping_err`), with `Direct_linear(w, t) = dose · (u_t · w) / r_c`, `rmsnorm_shift(w, t) = (u_t · h_i) · (1/r_i - 1/r_c)`, and `frozen_direct_effect(w, t) = dose · (u_t · w) / r_i`.
- **Frozen R-lens forecast:** `pred_dlogit(w, t) = dose · (u_t^T R_L w) / r_c`.
- **Realized downstream retention:** `Retention(w, t) = (exact_dlogit - rmsnorm_shift) / Direct_linear`, defined on the 4 non-null arms (`R_mf`, `R_ridge`, `logit_lens`, `direct_matched`; set to `null` on `rand_iso(t)` and wrong-target `R_mf(t')` on `t`). **Pre-data positivity guarantee:** Across all `7 × 16 = 112` `(L, t)` cells in `c0_prereg_constants.json`, `min(c) = 0.1730 > 0` and `min(cos_u_Rridge) = 0.2836 > 0`, so `u_t · w > 0` holds strictly for `112/112` cells with zero outcome-dependent missingness. Subtracting `rmsnorm_shift` makes `Retention(logit_lens) = (u_t · Δh_final) / (dose · ||u_t||) = û_t^T J û_t` exact.
- **Frozen predicted retention:** `pred_ret(w, t) = (u_t^T R_L w) / (u_t · w)` (`N/c` for `R_mf`, `pred_ret_Rridge` for `R_ridge`, `c·N` for `logit_lens` and `direct_matched`, where `N = ||R_L^T u_t|| / ||u_t||`, `c = cos(u_t, R_L^T u_t)`).
- **Per-trace specificity & KL (exact match to `run_olmo3_idea2_and_idea4.py:335-362`):** Computed per individual `(L, arm, target, prompt)` trace over the full vocabulary `V` in `fp32` before averaging across `(L, prompt)`: with `Δ = logits_int - logits_clean`, `dlogit_z = (Δ[t] - mean_V(Δ)) / max(std_V(Δ), 1e-8)` (unbiased sample `std_V`, `ddof = 1`, matching `torch.Tensor.std()`), and `KL = Σ_{v∈V} p_c(v) · (log p_c(v) - log p_i(v))` where `p_c = softmax(logits_clean)`, `p_i = softmax(logits_int)`.
- **Direct unembedding comparator (`ρ_direct`):** Within-`(arm, L)` Spearman correlation between `Direct_linear(w, t)` and `exact_dlogit(w, t)` across the 16 targets, Fisher-z averaged over the same cells as `ρ_within`.
- **Standard median & quantile convention:** Every median uses Python `statistics.median` (average of 8th and 9th order statistics for `n = 16`); every quantile `p_q` uses linear interpolation at index `q · (n - 1)` (matching `numpy.quantile(..., method="linear")`).
- **`CancR` (descriptive, unfiltered):** `(|Direct_linear| + Σ_l |attn_l| + Σ_l |mlp_l| + |rmsnorm_shift|) / max(|exact_dlogit|, 1e-6)`, reported unfiltered (`median`, `IQR`, `p90`, `max`, `mean`, and `ratio-of-means`).

## 3. Experimental Arms & Matched Regimes

For every layer `L ∈ {6, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27}`:
1. **`R_mf(t)`** (`16` targets): `normalize(R_L^T u_t)`. Each forward pass outputs the full `[V]` logit vector; in addition to scoring target `t`, we read **`R_mf_cross(t)`** directly from the **12 cross-domain wrong-target runs `R_mf(t')`** (`domain(t') != domain(t)`) at zero extra forward cost (3 same-domain wrong targets also logged descriptively).
2. **`R_ridge(t)`** (`16` targets): `normalize((R_L^T R_L + λI)^{-1} R_L^T u_t)`.
3. **`logit_lens(t)`** (`16` targets): `normalize(u_t)`.
4. **`direct_matched(t)`** (`16` targets × `K = 4` seeded orthogonal draws averaged across draws): `c · û_t + sqrt(1 - c^2) · z_perp` where `z_perp ⟂ {û_t, R_mf, R_ridge}`. Identical write norm and identical `Direct_linear` as `R_mf`, and identical linear R-lens forecast (`3cN` in Regime D) as `logit_lens`.
5. **`rand_iso`** (**`M = 32` seeded isotropic unit draws per layer `L`**, shared across all 16 targets):
   - On all 16 target readouts `u_t`: `Retention` and `CancR` set to `null`; measures the same-run noise floor on Regime-N traces (`512` `(m, t)` pairs per layer).
   - On each draw's own self-projection `v_self = rand_iso`: directly measures the real model's end-to-end Jacobian diagonal `v^T J v = (v · Δh_final) / dose` (`CancR_self` logged purely descriptively).

**Two Regimes:**
- **Regime N (Equal Write Norm):** `dose_N = 0.75 · post_mlp_norm[L]` (clean prompt-mean `post_norm` residual norm measured on the 8 clean baselines and logged in the receipt) across all 7 layers for arms `1–5`.
- **Regime D (Equal Local Direct Gain, gated band `L ∈ {12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27}`; `L6` descriptive):** `R_mf`, `R_ridge`, `logit_lens`, and `direct_matched` scaled so `Direct_linear = +3.0` logits (capped at `3.0 · post_mlp_norm[L]`). Capped traces are **dropped**; `cap_hit_rate <= 2%` per `(arm, L)` on `L12–L27`.

## 4. Exact Inference, Multiplicity Control & Pre-Registered Gates / Predictions

- **Inference & 4-Test Holm-Bonferroni Family (`α = 0.05`, `α/4 = 0.0125`):** 10,000-resample **BCa cluster bootstrap** resampling the **16 target clusters** (preserving crossed prompt & layer dependence; the 16 targets are `4 × 4` stratified across 4 domains — treated as exchangeable target clusters for overall transport inference, with any domain-level contrast treated as stratified/exploratory). Exact permutation/sign-flip p-values `p_1..p_4` control familywise Type-I error on the directional existence nulls across the 4 primary predictions (`C0.1–C0.4`), while the pre-registered magnitude/calibration thresholds act as minimum-effect gates. Every `p_1..p_4` has `p_min <= 1e-4 << 0.0125`.
- **Registered `2 × 2` Format × Style Moderation Diagnostics (non-gating):** Recompute `ρ_within` (`C0.1a`) and pooled `L12–L27` `gap / pred_gap_D3` (`C0.2a`) separately in **Bare (4 prompts)** vs. **ChatML (4 prompts)** and **Narrative (4 prompts)** vs. **Analytical (4 prompts)**, reporting Bare-minus-ChatML and Narrative-minus-Analytical contrasts with 95% BCa CIs over the 16 target clusters so any ChatML-prepend format dependence is exposed explicitly.
