- **Why `positions="all"` uses unscaled per-position `dose` (not `dose / T`):**
  - `R_out` (`transport.py:5`, fitted on `T = 256` WikiText-103 documents) sums `Σ_{t <= t'} ∂h_final(t') / ∂h_L(t)` with unit weight `1.0` at every source position `t`, matching `Δh_L(t) = dose · w` for all `t ∈ {0..T-1}`. Because softmax attention weights satisfy `Σ_t α_{T-1, t} = 1`, value transport `Σ_t α_{T-1, t} W_O W_V (dose · w)` is a **convex combination** (`O(1)` in `T`, not `O(T)`), and `Direct_linear = dose · (u_t · w) / r_c = +3.0` at `T-1` remains mode-invariant. Scaling by `dose / T` would shrink both `Direct_linear` and softmax value transport by `1 / T` (`7–43×`), breaking Regime-D matching and `R_out` calibration.
  - **Explicit total-norm & truncation disclosures:** (a) While per-position norm `dose` is identical across modes (`<= 3 · post_mlp_norm[L]`), total sequence-summed injected norm under `"all"` is `T · dose` (Frobenius `√T · dose`).
