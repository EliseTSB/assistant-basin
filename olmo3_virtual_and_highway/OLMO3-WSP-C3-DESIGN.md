## 1. Research Question & Prior Context

Stage C0 (`c96ebfdb`) and C0b (`112450e4`) mapped OLMo 3 7B's linear transport band, while Stage C1 (`45b9208c`) and Stage C2 (`4e277a20`) failed their global pre-run gates (`C1.0.3` anchor drift; `C2.0.2` unpassable upstream-patch telescoping gate) and held all primary confirmatory claims closed (`BLOCKED_BY_C1.0` / `BLOCKED_BY_C2.0`). Two empirical observations from C2's exploratory diagnostics motivate the mechanism tested here: (i) mean-centered unembeddings (`u_t - μ_U`) reduce common-mode bleed cleanly (`2.05×` bleed reduction at `94.1%` gain retention) without diagonal shrinkage; and (ii) OLMo 3 7B's `post_feedforward_layernorm` has `1–3` genuinely near-zero `|γ|` dimensions (`< 0.005`) per layer (`L21: 3`, `L24: 2`, `L27: 1`), so masking the `k = 4` smallest `|γ|` dimensions lifts `pre_norm` deliverable efficiency to `~100%` (`9.15×`, `1.45×`, `1.95×` on Pirate lexicon writes; `10.59×`, `2.05×`, `29.50×` on Pure-Markdown anti-writes at `0 CU`, with `cos_ret >= 0.9993`). As a **design assumption (not separately tested by a `K = 1` arm here)**, Stage C3 writes simultaneously to `K = 3` layers (`L21, L24, L27`) without re-orthogonalization, informed by C2's exploratory blocked readout (`EQ-C2.4c`, `+0.73%` median relative interaction at `0.5×`, which C2 explicitly noted did not license skipping re-orthogonalization as a confirmed theorem).

**Core Scientific Question (Stage C3 / `WSP v1`):**
1. **Pillar A (Primary — Compiled Persona vs. Lexical Word-Stuffing Under a Fixed Symmetric Ban):** Can a **zero-hook, zero-gradient literal rank-1 weight edit** into `mlp.down_proj.weight` across `L ∈ {21, 24, 27}` (low-`γ` `pre_norm` masked) compile a coherent **Pirate persona** into `Olmo-3-7B-Instruct` that beats a **sharp-null 3-seed Frobenius-norm-matched random control** (`Rand_Pirate_{s0,s1,s2}`, Part 173 control) on `N = 40` held-out identity & implied-content prompts **under a symmetric, leak-proof `common_pirate_ban` + surface-stem rejection** (`H1`) while preserving math, Python code, and neutral-chat `off_KL` (`EQ-C3.2`)? And at equal calibrated dose **`d_common = min(d*_con, d*_lex)`**, does closed-form **contrastive compilation** (`WSP_Pirate_Contrastive`, `Steer2Edit`-style mean residual difference on `16` disjoint calibration prompts) outperform pure `0`-rollout **lexicon-lens compilation** (`WSP_Pirate_Lexicon`) under that same fixed ban (`H2`)?
2. **Pillar B (Secondary — Mechanical No-Markdown Compilation, Never Combined with Persona):** Can the same zero-hook literal `pre_norm` weight-edit mechanism compile a 100% mechanically scored **No-Markdown rule** (`WSP_NoMD_Contrastive` in `H3`, and pure `0`-rollout `WSP_NoMD_Lexicon` in `H4`) on `NOMD_24` (`N = 24`) that reduces list-length-immune **`construct_types ∈ {0..7}`** vs. matched 3-seed random controls, cuts arm-side **`has_markdown`** prevalence vs. both `Plain` and `Rand_NoMD`, and maintains comparable answer length (`mean_tokens(Arm)/mean_tokens(Rand_mean) ∈ [0.85, 1.15]`, `M1`), while passing `EQ-C3.2`?

## 2. Literal Zero-Hook Weight-Space Compilation (`mlp.down_proj.weight`, `L ∈ {21, 24, 27}`)

`Olmo3ForCausalLM` is bias-free (`attention_bias = false`, no `down_proj.bias`), with post-norm residual updates:
$$h_{\text{out}}^{(L)} = h_{\text{mid}}^{(L)} + \text{RMSNorm}_{\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)}}\!\big(W_{\text{down}}^{(L)} m_L(x)\big), \qquad m_L(x) = \text{SiLU}(W_{\text{gate}}^{(L)} h_{\text{mid}}^{(L)}) \odot (W_{\text{up}}^{(L)} h_{\text{mid}}^{(L)}) \in \mathbb{R}^{11008}.$$

### 2.1 Calibration Quantities (Computed Strictly on Disjoint `calib_16`, Never Evaluated)
On the `16` disjoint calibration prompts (`CALIB_PROMPTS_16`, greedy `32` tokens, `Plain`, `SYS_PIRATE`, and `SYS_NOMD`), record at each layer `L ∈ {21, 24, 27}` across the assistant turn-start token (`<|im_start|>assistant\n`) plus the first `16` generated tokens:
1. **Persistent MLP Key $\hat{k}_L \in \mathbb{R}^{11008}$:** Mean plain MLP intermediate activation $\bar{m}_L = \text{mean}(m_L(x))$, unit key $\hat{k}_L = \bar{m}_L / \|\bar{m}_L\|_2$, scalar mean key projection $\bar{c}_L = \text{mean}(\hat{k}_L^\top m_L(x)) > 0$, and key firing stability $\text{CV}_L = \text{std}(\hat{k}_L^\top m_L) / \bar{c}_L$ (gated in `C3.0.2`: `CV_L <= 1.0`, plus max per-token delivery ratio `max(k̂_L^T m_L) / c̄_L` logged in receipt).
2. **Clean Layer Norms:** Mean clean post-MLP output norm $\text{pmn}[L] = \text{mean}(\|\text{RMSNorm}_{\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)}}(W_{\text{down}}^{(L)} m_L)\|_2)$ and mean clean pre-norm MLP RMS $\text{rms\_pre}[L] = \text{mean}(\text{rms}(W_{\text{down}}^{(L)} m_L))$.
3. **Contrastive Target Directions ($v_{\text{post}}^{(L)}$):** For `WSP_Pirate_Contrastive`, $\Delta h_L = \text{mean}(h_{\text{out}}^{(L,\text{SYS\_PIRATE})} - h_{\text{out}}^{(L,\text{Plain})})$ across `calib_16`, normalized to unit norm. Identically for `WSP_NoMD_Contrastive` using `SYS_NOMD`. Each compiled arm's mask quality (`cos_ret`, `eff_ratio`) is gated **per-direction** in `C3.0_direction_quality` (`cos_ret >= 0.97` across `L21/24/27`, `M7`; failure returns `DIRECTION_INVALID` only for hypotheses depending on that arm, and `|cos_ret(Con, L) - cos_ret(Lex, L)|` is logged beside `H2`).

### 2.2 Low-`γ` `pre_norm` Masking, Pinned `bf16` Storage Retention & Sharp-Null Random Control
Given any unit post-norm target direction $v_{\text{post}}^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^{4096}$, let $S_L$ be the frozen `drop_k = 4` smallest $|\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)}|$ coordinates (`L21: [1074, 1483, 3125, 3462]`, `L24: [1042, 1745, 2632, 3807]`, `L27: [726, 1745, 2632, 3885]`). Compute:
$$v_{\text{pre\_raw}}^{(L)} = \text{mask}_{S_L}\!\left(\frac{v_{\text{post}}^{(L)}}{\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)}}\right), \qquad \hat{v}_{\text{pre}}^{(L)} = \frac{v_{\text{pre\_raw}}^{(L)}}{\|v_{\text{pre\_raw}}^{(L)}\|_2}.$$
For any total dose $d$ in the 6-rung ladder (`DOSE_LADDER = [0.075, 0.15, 0.30, 0.45, 0.60, 0.80]`), split equally across `K = 3` layers ($\delta_L = (d/3)\cdot \text{pmn}[L]$, calibrated at the mean token projection $\bar{c}_L$):
$$\alpha_L(d) = \frac{\delta_L \cdot \text{rms\_pre}[L]}{\bar{c}_L \cdot \|\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)} \odot \hat{v}_{\text{pre}}^{(L)}\|_2}, \qquad \Delta W_{\text{down}}^{(L)}(d) = \alpha_L(d)\,\hat{v}_{\text{pre}}^{(L)}\,\hat{k}_L^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{4096 \times 11008}.$$

- **Zero Hooks, `fp32`-Master Application, Bitwise Restore & Gated `bf16` Storage Retention (`B2/C2/F1`):** Keep clean `fp32` master copies of the three target `down_proj.weight` tensors (`541 MB` total) and apply edits via `W_edited_bf16 = (W_clean_fp32 + ΔW_fp32).to(bf16)`, restoring from `W_clean_bf16` after every arm with bitwise `torch.equal(W_restored, W_clean)`. Define the two pinned per-layer storage-retention quantities (`edit_spec.bf16_storage_retention`):
  $$\Delta W_{\text{stored}}^{(L)} := W_{\text{edited\_bf16}}^{(L)}.\text{float}() - W_{\text{clean\_bf16}}^{(L)}.\text{float}(), \qquad \text{stored\_frob\_ret}_L := \frac{\|\Delta W_{\text{stored}}^{(L)}\|_F}{\|\Delta W_{\text{intended}}^{(L)}\|_F}, \qquad \text{stored\_rank1\_proj\_ret}_L := \frac{\langle \Delta W_{\text{stored}}^{(L)},\, \hat{v}_{\text{pre}}^{(L)} \hat{k}_L^\top\rangle}{\alpha_L}.$$
  Reproducibly audited inside [`build_olmo3_c3_constants.py`](build_olmo3_c3_constants.py) (`bf16_retention_audit_0cu`, `BF16_AUDIT_KEY_SEED = 2026112604`) on the real cached `Olmo-3-7B-Instruct` `L21/24/27` `down_proj.weight` (`abs_mean ≈ 0.0182`): `α = 1e-4` loses `99.9%` of rank-1 projection; **`sham_alpha = 0.05`** deterministically modifies `5.48–5.92%` of entries (`stored_frob_ret = 0.643–0.721`, `stored_rank1_proj_ret = 0.313–0.412`); `α = 0.10` hits `stored_frob_ret = 0.828–0.852`, `stored_rank1_proj_ret = 0.532–0.593`; `α = 0.50` hits `0.956–0.957`; `α = 1.00` hits `0.992–0.993`. **Hard gating (`C2/F1`):** A ladder rung `d` is selectable in `C3.0_commit_gate` **ONLY IF** every layer `L ∈ {21, 24, 27}` satisfies **`stored_rank1_proj_ret >= 0.50` AND `stored_frob_ret >= 0.80`** (otherwise marked `dose_unrepresentable = True` and fails the commit gate). Whenever any arm's selected `d*` has `stored_rank1_proj_ret < 0.90`, the receipt reports the full per-arm retention table and delivered-dose ratios `ret(Con)/ret(Rand_mean)` and `ret(Con)/ret(Lex)` beside `H1–H4`.
- **Sharp-Null 3-Seed Random Control (`Rand_Pirate_{s0,s1,s2}`, `Rand_NoMD_{s0,s1,s2}`):** Uses `3` frozen isotropic unit vectors $\hat{v}_{\text{rand},s}^{(L)}$ (`s ∈ {0, 1, 2}`, seeded `BASE + 100*L + s`) zeroed on $S_L$, the exact same $\hat{k}_L$, and the **exact same scalar $\alpha_L(d^*)$** at **every distinct `d*` in use**. Exact intended Frobenius match (`||ΔW_rand||_F = ||ΔW_edit||_F`; stored bf16 ratio logged). For the **frozen lexicon directions**, delivered post-norm ratio `rms_γ / ||γ ⊙ v̂_pre||` is `0.9946 / 0.9947 / 0.9688` (Pirate) and `0.9955 / 0.9930 / 0.9674` (NoMD) across `L21 / L24 / L27` (`0.45–3.26%` smaller delivered shift for randoms); for the **live contrastive directions**, delivered-match ratio is logged live and bounded by `cos_ret >= 0.97` (`<= ~3%` in either direction, `F10`). All `6` random controls receive `RESERVE_PROMPTS_8` `off_KL` checks (`<= 0.35` median gated in `C3.0.2`, `M8`), with `Rand_*_s0` designated as the capability proxy for `s1/s2`. **Estimand note:** Sharp null against these `3` frozen seed draws (population generalization over arbitrary random directions is not claimed).

### 2.4 Pinned `off_KL` Estimator & Binding Disjoint-Reserve Commit Gate (`C3.0_commit_gate`)
- **Pinned `off_KL` Estimator (nats/token):** For each prompt $p$ with `Plain`'s `48`-token greedy reply $y^{(p)}_{1:T_p}$ ($T_p \le 48$), compute teacher-forced forward logits at **`B = 1`, `use_cache=False`**, cast to `float32` log-softmax, and compute per-position KL $\text{kl}_{p,t} = \sum_v p_{\text{plain}}(v|x_{<t})\log(p_{\text{plain}}(v|x_{<t})/p_{\text{arm}}(v|x_{<t}))$ and per-prompt mean $\text{off\_KL}(p; \text{arm}) = \frac{1}{T_p}\sum_{t=1}^{T_p}\text{kl}_{p,t}$ [nats/token].
- **Binding Reserve Commit Gate (`RESERVE_PROMPTS_8`, `6` rungs, `0` test peeking, `C2/F1/M2`):** For each of the `4` compiled arms, evaluate all `6` ladder rungs `d ∈ [0.075, 0.15, 0.30, 0.45, 0.60, 0.80]`. Select the **highest selectable rung `d*`** satisfying ALL FOUR conditions:
  1. **bf16 Representability (`C2/F1`):** Every layer `L ∈ {21, 24, 27}` has `stored_rank1_proj_ret >= 0.50` AND `stored_frob_ret >= 0.80` (else `dose_unrepresentable = True`, fails commit gate), AND
  2. **Per-Prompt Median & Max `off_KL`:** Reserve median `off_KL <= 0.25` nats/token AND reserve max prompt `off_KL <= 0.60` nats/token (`0.25` nats/token is a pre-data calibration choice with no prior-stage source — distinct from Stage C2's single-position KL bar where C2's upstream-patched control scored `0.3417` and failed, `M2`), AND
  3. **Per-Position Collapse Guard (`M2`):** Max single-position KL across all `8 × 48` positions `max_{p,t} kl_{p,t} <= 4.0` nats, AND
  4. **Reserve Fluency:** Reserve broken rate `== 0 / 8`.
- **Hard Fallback Consequence (`DOSE_INVALID`):** If no rung passes, log `commit_gate_fallback = true`, record **`DOSE_INVALID`** for every confirmatory hypothesis depending on that arm, and report its 6-rung reserve & retention curve descriptively. An all-`DOSE_INVALID` sweep is explicitly accepted as an honest pre-registered outcome.
- **Random & Cross-Arm Pairing:** `Rand_Pirate_{s0,s1,s2}` and `Rand_NoMD_{s0,s1,s2}` are evaluated at **every distinct `d*` in use**. `H2` compares `WSP_Pirate_Contrastive` vs. `WSP_Pirate_Lexicon` at matched dose **`d_common = min(d*_con, d*_lex)`**, with **`EQ-C3.2` gating on `Con@d_common` and logged-only on `Lex@d_common`** (identically pinned in JSON and §5, `F2`).

## 5. Confirmatory Holm-4 Family (`α = 0.05`), Exact Step-Down, Ordered Verdicts & Point-Bar Power

- **Exact Holm-Bonferroni Step-Down Multiplicity Control (`C1`, implemented in [`c3_scoring.py`](c3_scoring.py)::`holm_reject()`):** Compute one-sided paired sign-flip permutation p-values over independent prompt units (`100,000` draws, `PERM_SEED = 2026112601`) for all `4` hypotheses. Sort ascending by `(p_value, hyp_id)`. Reject $h_{(1)}$ iff $p_{(1)} \le 0.05/4 = 0.0125$; reject $h_{(k)}$ iff **all previous ranks $1\dots(k-1)$ were rejected** AND $p_{(k)} \le 0.05/(5-k)$; **stop at the first failure** so all unreached hypotheses have `holm_sig = False`. `90%` paired BCa intervals (`BCA_SEED = 2026112602`) are reported descriptively.
