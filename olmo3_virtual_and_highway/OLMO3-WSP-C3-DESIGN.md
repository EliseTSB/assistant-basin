## 1. Research Question & Prior Context

Stage C0 (`c96ebfdb`) and C0b (`112450e4`) mapped OLMo 3 7B's linear transport band, while Stage C1 (`45b9208c`) and Stage C2 (`4e277a20`) failed their global pre-run gates (`C1.0.3` anchor drift; `C2.0.2` unpassable upstream-patch telescoping gate) and held all primary confirmatory claims closed (`BLOCKED_BY_C1.0` / `BLOCKED_BY_C2.0`). Two empirical observations from C2's exploratory diagnostics motivate the mechanism tested here: (i) mean-centered unembeddings (`u_t - μ_U`) reduce common-mode bleed cleanly (`2.05×` bleed reduction at `94.1%` gain retention) without diagonal shrinkage; and (ii) OLMo 3 7B's `post_feedforward_layernorm` has `1–3` genuinely near-zero `|γ|` dimensions (`< 0.005`) per layer (`L21: 3`, `L24: 2`, `L27: 1`), so masking the `k = 4` smallest `|γ|` dimensions lifts `pre_norm` deliverable efficiency to `~100%` (`9.15×`, `1.45×`, `1.95×` on Pirate lexicon writes; `10.59×`, `2.05×`, `29.50×` on Pure-Markdown anti-writes at `0 CU`, with `cos_ret >= 0.9993`). As a **design assumption (not separately tested by a `K = 1` arm here)**, Stage C3 writes simultaneously to `K = 3` layers (`L21, L24, L27`) without re-orthogonalization, informed by C2's exploratory blocked readout (`EQ-C2.4c`, `+0.73%` median relative interaction at `0.5×`, which C2 explicitly noted did not license skipping re-orthogonalization as a confirmed theorem).

**Core Scientific Question (Stage C3 / `WSP v1`):**
1. **Pillar A (Primary — Compiled Persona vs. Lexical Word-Stuffing Under a Fixed Symmetric Ban):** Can a **zero-hook, zero-gradient literal rank-1 weight edit** into `mlp.down_proj.weight` across `L ∈ {21, 24, 27}` (low-`γ` `pre_norm` masked) compile a coherent **Pirate persona** into `Olmo-3-7B-Instruct` that beats a **sharp-null 3-seed Frobenius-norm-matched random control** (`Rand_Pirate_{s0,s1,s2}`, Part 173 control) on `N = 40` held-out identity & implied-content prompts **under a symmetric, leak-proof `common_pirate_ban` + surface-stem rejection** (`H1`) while preserving math, Python code, and neutral-chat `off_KL` (`EQ-C3.2`)? And at equal calibrated dose **`d_common = min(d*_con, d*_lex)`**, does closed-form **contrastive compilation** (`WSP_Pirate_Contrastive`, `Steer2Edit`-style mean residual difference on `16` disjoint calibration prompts) outperform pure `0`-rollout **lexicon-lens compilation** (`WSP_Pirate_Lexicon`) under that same fixed ban (`H2`)?
2. **Pillar B (Secondary — Mechanical No-Markdown Compilation, Never Combined with Persona):** Can the same zero-hook literal `pre_norm` weight-edit mechanism compile a 100% mechanically scored **No-Markdown rule** (`WSP_NoMD_Contrastive` in `H3`, and pure `0`-rollout `WSP_NoMD_Lexicon` in `H4`) on `NOMD_24` (`N = 24`) that reduces list-length-immune **`construct_types ∈ {0..7}`** vs. matched 3-seed random controls AND cuts arm-side **`has_markdown`** prevalence vs. both `Plain` and `Rand_NoMD`, while passing `EQ-C3.2`?

## 2. Literal Zero-Hook Weight-Space Compilation (`mlp.down_proj.weight`, `L ∈ {21, 24, 27}`)

`Olmo3ForCausalLM` is bias-free (`attention_bias = false`, no `down_proj.bias`), with post-norm residual updates:
$$h_{\text{out}}^{(L)} = h_{\text{mid}}^{(L)} + \text{RMSNorm}_{\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)}}\!\big(W_{\text{down}}^{(L)} m_L(x)\big), \qquad m_L(x) = \text{SiLU}(W_{\text{gate}}^{(L)} h_{\text{mid}}^{(L)}) \odot (W_{\text{up}}^{(L)} h_{\text{mid}}^{(L)}) \in \mathbb{R}^{11008}.$$

### 2.1 Calibration Quantities (Computed Strictly on Disjoint `calib_16`, Never Evaluated)
On the `16` disjoint calibration prompts (`CALIB_PROMPTS_16`, greedy `32` tokens, `Plain`, `SYS_PIRATE`, and `SYS_NOMD`), record at each layer `L ∈ {21, 24, 27}` across the assistant turn-start token (`<|im_start|>assistant\n`) plus the first `16` generated tokens:
1. **Persistent MLP Key $\hat{k}_L \in \mathbb{R}^{11008}$:** Mean plain MLP intermediate activation $\bar{m}_L = \text{mean}(m_L(x))$, unit key $\hat{k}_L = \bar{m}_L / \|\bar{m}_L\|_2$, scalar mean key projection $\bar{c}_L = \text{mean}(\hat{k}_L^\top m_L(x)) > 0$, and key firing stability $\text{CV}_L = \text{std}(\hat{k}_L^\top m_L) / \bar{c}_L$ (gated in `C3.0.2`: `CV_L <= 1.0`, plus max per-token delivery ratio `max(k̂_L^T m_L) / c̄_L` logged in receipt).
2. **Clean Layer Norms:** Mean clean post-MLP output norm $\text{pmn}[L] = \text{mean}(\|\text{RMSNorm}_{\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)}}(W_{\text{down}}^{(L)} m_L)\|_2)$ and mean clean pre-norm MLP RMS $\text{rms\_pre}[L] = \text{mean}(\text{rms}(W_{\text{down}}^{(L)} m_L))$.
3. **Contrastive Target Directions ($v_{\text{post}}^{(L)}$):** For `WSP_Pirate_Contrastive`, $\Delta h_L = \text{mean}(h_{\text{out}}^{(L,\text{SYS\_PIRATE})} - h_{\text{out}}^{(L,\text{Plain})})$ across `calib_16`, normalized to unit norm. Identically for `WSP_NoMD_Contrastive` using `SYS_NOMD`. Both contrastive directions' mask quality (`cos_ret`, `eff_ratio`) are computed live and gated in `C3.0.2` (`cos_ret >= 0.95` across all 4 compiled arms and 3 layers).

### 2.2 Low-`γ` `pre_norm` Masking & Exact Frobenius-Norm-Matched Rank-1 Edit
Given any unit post-norm target direction $v_{\text{post}}^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^{4096}$, let $S_L$ be the frozen `drop_k = 4` smallest $|\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)}|$ coordinates (`L21: [1074, 1483, 3125, 3462]`, `L24: [1042, 1745, 2632, 3807]`, `L27: [726, 1745, 2632, 3885]`). Compute:
$$v_{\text{pre\_raw}}^{(L)} = \text{mask}_{S_L}\!\left(\frac{v_{\text{post}}^{(L)}}{\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)}}\right), \qquad \hat{v}_{\text{pre}}^{(L)} = \frac{v_{\text{pre\_raw}}^{(L)}}{\|v_{\text{pre\_raw}}^{(L)}\|_2}.$$
For any total dose $d$ in the 5-rung ladder (`DOSE_LADDER = [0.15, 0.30, 0.45, 0.60, 0.80]`), split equally across `K = 3` layers ($\delta_L = (d/3)\cdot \text{pmn}[L]$, calibrated at the mean token projection $\bar{c}_L$):
$$\alpha_L(d) = \frac{\delta_L \cdot \text{rms\_pre}[L]}{\bar{c}_L \cdot \|\gamma_{\text{ff}}^{(L)} \odot \hat{v}_{\text{pre}}^{(L)}\|_2}, \qquad \Delta W_{\text{down}}^{(L)}(d) = \alpha_L(d)\,\hat{v}_{\text{pre}}^{(L)}\,\hat{k}_L^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{4096 \times 11008}.$$

- **Zero Hooks & Bitwise Restore:** Applied literally via `model.layers[L].mlp.down_proj.weight.data.add_(ΔW_L.to(bf16))` and restored cleanly from a saved bf16 clean copy after every arm, verified via bitwise `torch.equal(W_restored, W_clean)`.
- **Sharp-Null 3-Seed Random Control (`Rand_Pirate_{s0,s1,s2}`, `Rand_NoMD_{s0,s1,s2}`):** Uses `3` frozen isotropic unit vectors $\hat{v}_{\text{rand},s}^{(L)}$ (`s ∈ {0, 1, 2}`, seeded `BASE + 100*L + s`) zeroed on the exact same $S_L$, the exact same key $\hat{k}_L$, and the **exact same scalar $\alpha_L(d^*)$** at **every distinct `d*` in use**. This guarantees exact weight-space Frobenius norm match ($\|\Delta W_{\text{rand}}^{(L)}\|_F = \|\Delta W_{\text{edit}}^{(L)}\|_F = |\alpha_L(d^*)|$) and approximate delivered post-norm match (`rms_γ / ||γ ⊙ v̂_pre||` = `0.9946 / 0.9947 / 0.9688` at `L21 / L24 / L27`, i.e., `0.5–3.1%` smaller delivered shift for randoms, logged explicitly in the receipt). **Estimand note:** Statistical tests evaluate the sharp null against these `3` frozen seed draws (population generalization over arbitrary random directions is not claimed).

### 2.4 Pinned `off_KL` Estimator & Binding Disjoint-Reserve Commit Gate (`C3.0_commit_gate`)
- **Pinned `off_KL` Estimator (nats/token):** For each prompt $p$ with `Plain`'s `48`-token greedy reply $y^{(p)}_{1:T_p}$ ($T_p \le 48$), compute teacher-forced forward logits at **`B = 1`, `use_cache=False`**, cast to `float32` log-softmax, and take the **per-token mean** forward KL over generated answer positions only:
  $$\text{off\_KL}(p; \text{arm}) = \frac{1}{T_p}\sum_{t=1}^{T_p}\sum_{v=1}^{V} p_{\text{plain}}(v \mid x_{<t})\,\log\frac{p_{\text{plain}}(v \mid x_{<t})}{p_{\text{arm}}(v \mid x_{<t})}\quad [\text{nats/token}].$$
  Aggregate across prompts by **median** (with **max** also gated/logged).
- **Binding Reserve Commit Gate (`RESERVE_PROMPTS_8`, `0` test peeking):** For each of the `4` compiled arms (`WSP_Pirate_Contrastive`, `WSP_Pirate_Lexicon`, `WSP_NoMD_Contrastive`, `WSP_NoMD_Lexicon`), evaluate all `5` ladder rungs `d ∈ [0.15, 0.30, 0.45, 0.60, 0.80]` on `RESERVE_PROMPTS_8` (`off_KL` + `48`-token greedy broken check). Select the **highest rung `d*`** satisfying:
  1. Reserve median `off_KL <= 0.25` nats/token AND reserve max `off_KL <= 0.60` nats/token, AND
  2. Reserve broken rate `== 0 / 8`.
- **Hard Fallback Consequence (`DOSE_INVALID`):** If no rung passes for an arm, log `commit_gate_fallback = true`, record **`DOSE_INVALID`** for every confirmatory hypothesis depending on that arm (never silently scoring a failed-dose arm as a scientific null!), and report its 5-rung reserve curve descriptively.
- **Random & Cross-Arm Pairing:** `Rand_Pirate_{s0,s1,s2}` and `Rand_NoMD_{s0,s1,s2}` are evaluated at **every distinct `d*` in use** (reused only when two arms select the exact same rung). `H2` compares `WSP_Pirate_Contrastive` vs. `WSP_Pirate_Lexicon` at matched dose **`d_common = min(d*_con, d*_lex)`** (isolating direction mechanism at equal calibrated dose; the pipeline operating-point contrast at `d*_con` vs. `d*_lex` is reported as secondary `D7`).

## 3. Disjoint Evaluation Batteries (`13` Splits, `216` Unique Prompts) & Scoring

`verify_disjoint_prompts()` enforces `0` exact-string overlap across **all `13` splits** (`16` calib, `8` reserve, `20` identity [`10` direct + `10` indirect], `20` implied, `12 × 3 = 36` multi-turn, `12 + 12 = 24` copula self/stranger, `24` markdown-eliciting, `20` math, `12` Python code, `8` refusal, `16` neutral chat, and **`12` completely fresh disjoint `READER_GOLD_12` prompts**), `0` overlap with `c0/c1/c2_prereg_constants.json` AND `prompts.py`, and **`0` Pirate ban stem occurrences** in `reserve_8`, `identity_20`, `implied_20`, all 3 turns of `multiturn_12`, and `neutral_chat_16` (`res_01` `"compass"` replaced with `"suspended bar magnet"`).

- **Symmetric, Leak-Proof Primary Ban (`common_pirate_ban` + `surface_stem_reject`):** Applied **identically to every arm** (`Plain`, `Sys_Pirate`, `WSP_*`, `Rand_*`) in `H1` and `H2`. Covers `79` focused pirate & maritime-role/vessel/slang stems (`PIRATE_BAN_STEMS`, dropping overly generic nature words `gold, island, waves, tide, port, bow, stern`), enforced at decode via:
  1. **`95` frozen single-token IDs** (`logit = -inf`), AND
  2. **`380` frozen multi-token `bad_words_ids` sequences** (blocking every multi-piece pirate word including `ahoy, matey, avast, arrr, yarr, argh, shiver, timbers, blimey, buccaneer, corsair, cutlass, doubloon, galleon, schooner, sloop, frigate, mutiny, parrot, jolly, grog, landlubber, swashbuckler` across lowercase/Title/UPPER with and without leading space), PLUS
  3. **Pure-stdlib `surface_stem_regex` rejection** at scoring time (`\b(?:...)\b`, case-insensitive): any banned-regime generation still containing a `PIRATE_BAN_STEMS` word automatically scores `strict_pirate = 0` (and triggers a `C3.0.2` audit warning).
- **Secondary `D6` Adaptive Part 168 Crutch-Removal Ban (`DEMO_SHOWCASE_IDS_12` only):** Adds each edited arm's own top-50 strictly positive non-special tokens ranked by `mean_{p ∈ calib_16, t ∈ plain_ans_32}(logit_arm - logit_plain)` outside `common_pirate_ban` (ties broken by ascending `token_id`; `< 50` positive → keep all positive; `Plain` has `own = ∅`), alongside a **matched-donor ban** (`Con` evaluated under `Rand_Pirate_s0`'s top-50 + `common_pirate_ban`) to separate crutch-removal from generic 50-token ban fluency cost.
- **Mechanical `is_broken(text)` (pure-stdlib, frozen):** Empty/whitespace-only, `< 3` alphabetic chars, contains `U+FFFD` or `"!!!!"`, any 3-word n-gram repeated `>= 4` times, or any 5-word n-gram repeated `>= 2` times.
- **Mechanical `construct_types(text) ∈ {0..7}` & `has_markdown(text)` (pure-stdlib, frozen):** Presence indicator (`0/1`) for each of `7` distinct markdown construct families: (1) ATX headings (`^\s*#{1,6}\s+`), (2) bold/strong (`\*\*[^*\n]+\*\*` or `__[^_\n]+__`), (3) fenced code blocks (`` ``` ``), (4) unordered bullets (`^\s*[-*+]\s+`), (5) ordered lists (`^\s*\d+\.\s+`), (6) markdown table separator rows (`^\s*\|?[\s:-]+\|[\s|:-]*$`), (7) horizontal rules (`^\s*---+\s*$`). `construct_types = sum(present_7)` is **immune to list-length inflation**; `has_markdown = (construct_types >= 1)`. Raw hit count `markdown_hits` is also logged descriptively.
- **Mechanical `math_exact` & `code_pass`:** Exact final integer after `FINAL:` (fallback: last integer) on `MATH_20`; isolated `python3 -I` subprocess (`timeout = 3 s`, exit code `0` on all frozen asserts) on `CODE_12`.
- **Dual Blind Readers (`audit_c3_label.py`, Part 218 protocol):** Two independent readers (`deepseek-v4.1-flash` via `ocgo` and `gpt-6-luna` via `cliproxy`), completely blind to arm names, ban status, and each other; each sees only `(prompt, answer)` and outputs `{"pirate_persona": true|false, "coherent": true|false}`.
  - **Pre-Check Disjoint Gold Gate:** Both readers must score **`>= 10 / 12`** on disjoint `READER_GOLD_12` (`1` explicit positive, `5` subtle zero-pirate-stem positives, `6` negatives including 3rd-person false-friend and a zero-stem banned-regime word-stuffing loop). Gold accuracy is logged **separately for `subtle_zero_stem` (`5`) vs. `explicit` (`1`) vs. negatives (`6`)**.
  - **Per-Ban & Pooled Agreement Gate (`C3.R`):** Requires pooled Cohen's `κ >= 0.60` **AND per-ban `κ >= 0.55` separately on `ban=common_pirate_ban` and `ban=none`** (per-arm descriptive `κ` also logged in receipt). Per-item primary label: `strict_pirate = (reader1 AND reader2 AND (not is_broken) AND (ban=='none' OR not surface_stem_hit))`. Mean/median answer token length per `(arm, ban)` is logged alongside a length-matched robustness check (`S20`). If `C3.R` fails, ONLY reader-dependent verdicts (`H1, H2, D0, D5`) record `NOT_READ_LOW_KAPPA`; all mechanical verdicts (`H3, H4, EQ-C3.2, D1, D2`) remain fully readable!

## 5. Confirmatory Holm-4 Family (`α = 0.05`), Explicit Verdicts & `0 CU` Power Analysis

Every hypothesis uses a one-sided paired sign-flip permutation test over independent prompt units (`100,000` draws, `PERM_SEED = 2026112601`), controlled via Holm-Bonferroni (`m = 4`, `α_k = 0.05 / (5 - k)`). `90%` paired BCa intervals (`BCA_SEED = 2026112602`) are reported **descriptively** alongside exact point estimates (not as an unstable/redundant lattice gate).

**Explicit Verdict Enum (pinned in JSON):** `PASS`, `EFFECT_BELOW_BAR`, `FAIL`, `DOSE_INVALID`, `CAPABILITY_FAIL`, `NOT_READ_LOW_KAPPA`, `BLOCKED_BY_NOMD_ROOM`, `BLOCKED_BY_C3.0`.
- **Boolean Confirmation Rule (identical structure across `H1–H4`, exact formulas pinned in JSON):**
  - `PASS` iff `(all required gates PASS) AND (not commit_gate_fallback) AND (EQ-C3.2 PASS for treatment arm) AND (broken_bar) AND (holm_p <= alpha_k) AND (point_bar)`.
  - `EFFECT_BELOW_BAR` iff `(gates + dose + EQ-C3.2 + broken_bar PASS) AND (holm_p <= alpha_k) AND (not point_bar)` (separates statistically significant small effects from true nulls).
