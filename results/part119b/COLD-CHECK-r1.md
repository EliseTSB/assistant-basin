# Part 119b cold check, round 1 (Haiku 5.5 subagent, 2026-10-30)

**Findings, most important first**

1. **The title claims a headline the registered rule did not return.** README line 1: "At alpha 0.4, OLMo 2 1B's answers to 'Who are you?' mostly stay coherent under two pushes." README line 7 says the rule returns "No registered headline reading; report the numbers," and design rule (3) says nothing is read. The title reads 119b.1 as the finding, and its coherence half is one LLM reader on 108 answers (the second reader covered 30). It should state the numbers, for example: "At alpha 0.4, triage broke 33 of 741 pair answers; one LLM reader marked 100 of a 108-answer sample coherent; the registered headline rule returned no reading." It should also name the 39 persona directions. Evidence: `headline_by_rule`, scorer lines 1280–1304; design lines 158–162.

2. **Most 0.4 answers are generic assistant replies, and the README never says the persona is mostly absent.** The design says "at 0.4 most answers carry no persona to ask about" (Controls, reader bullet). Its pilot note and the README's pilot section say most 0.4 answers were "the plain assistant answer." Checking `answers.jsonl`: none of the 741 pair@0.4 answers is identical to the plain answer, 6 share its first 50 characters, none has word overlap of 0.6 or more with it, and 575 begin "I am" (673 of 741 at 0.2, 362 at 0.6). "Plain assistant answer" is never defined. It should be defined, the share of 0.4 answers that carry a persona should be given under a stated test, and the title should say that 0.4 coherence is mostly coherence of generic replies.

3. **Stopped predictions are shown without their outcomes, and the lead reads the dose pattern they block.** README lines 20, 21, 23 and 24 say "stopped by Gate 3 (not read as a dose effect)" but not what each would have produced. Against their limits: 119b.4 is 0.302 against at most 0.25 (fails). 119b.5 is 0.178 against at most half of 0.302, which is 0.151 (fails). 119b.7 is 0.2395 with interval 0.193–0.289, and 0.4 minus plain is 0.212 (would hold). 119b.8 is 0.077 with interval 0.026–0.127 (would hold). Line 5 meanwhile says the share "is lower at 0.2 (0.178) than at 0.8 (0.468)," the dose comparison the gate forbids. Line 93 says the same of TV. Each stopped row should give its would-be result, and the dose sentence should carry the Gate 3 qualifier.

4. **The 0.8 interaction share is on a different support from the one the README states.** README line 84 says shares use "positions resolved at all four doses." The 0.8 row (line 91) and line 5 give 0.467962 and 0.367972. Those are the all-positions medians (score119b.json, by_dose 0.8: `median_row_share_all_positions` and `median_row_share_resolved_at_dose`). The common-all-doses values in the same file are 0.472547 (real) and 0.370207 (control), and I reproduced both from `interaction.jsonl`. The 0.2, 0.4 and 0.6 values reproduce exactly on the common support, and so do the 0.8 slopes and norm medians. The design's Method says "Shared support, so that a change with dose comes from the dose and not from a different scoring set," which this mix breaks. NUMBERS line 41 repeats the common-support claim, and NUMBERS line 48 has the all-positions values. The figure's middle panel title says "on positions resolved at every dose." Use 0.473 and 0.370, or label 0.468 as all-positions and keep it in the Gate 2 row. No verdict changes.

5. **The breaking line is unscoped, and its comparative part is untested.** README line 7 adds "Breaking tracks how much is pushed more than which neurons are pushed" as a general claim. Singles, neuron against random at matched size (two-sided Fisher): 4/39 vs 1/39 at 0.8 (p = 0.36); 12/39 vs 9/39 at 1.13 (p = 0.61); 30/39 vs 25/39 at 1.6 (p = 0.32). Persona directions break 3 to 5 more of 39 at each size, none significant. For pairs, real 357/741 against random pairs 286/741 at 0.8 is a 9.6-point gap (p = 0.0002), and the README gives the random rate without the test. The dose evidence is large: real pairs 4.5% to 48.2% from 0.4 to 0.8; neuron singles 10.3% to 76.9% from 0.8 to 1.6; random singles 2.6% to 64.1%. So "how much" is supported by the pair dose series and the singles. The "more than which neurons" comparison is not measured as worded. The 10.050 norm has no unit, and `push_norms.json` holds combined norms only for pairs, so it cannot be compared with the 1.13 singles. The 49.0% against 48.0% split compares layers, not norms. The line was added on 119b.3 held by one answer (12 of 39, against 11.7 needed). It should be scoped to these 39 units on one question, and the size series with its neuron–random gaps and tests should be shown.

6. **The "floor remains open" hedge is weaker than the design's own error model.** README line 5 and line 229 say the 0.2 share "may be a numeric floor; nothing here tests that possibility." The design's Method says a share "at nPair 1.0 ... carries a floor of about 0.13, and at nPair 5 about 0.005." On the 19,241 common real positions at 0.2: 1.5% have nPair below 2 and 11% below 3. Among nPair of at least 3 the median share is 0.142 (17,103 positions); among nPair of at least 5 it is 0.115 (9,701). The floor at nPair 3 is about 0.014, so the floor does not explain most of 0.178. Controls at nPair of at least 3 give 0.120. The README should show this arithmetic. If the share is real at 0.2, Gate 3's premise of near-additivity at a small push fails for this model, which is a finding about the model and not only the instrument.

7. **Reader coherence is undefined, and its instruction is not quoted.** README lines 43 and 62–80 rest on the reader's coherent field. The prompt (`part119/label119.py` lines 95–96) says only "Also answer whether the answer is coherent," and the reader also received both persona descriptions. The README reports no human check of the labels. The 92.6% and the 0.4-to-0.8 contrast depend on that undefined judgment. The instruction should be quoted, the lack of definition stated, and the persona descriptions mentioned.

8. **The Gate 3 sentence is misquoted.** README line 5 quotes the design as "a share that does not fall at a small push cannot be told from a floor in the measure." Design lines 106–108 read "because a share that does not fall at a small push, or that falls only because the effects fell below rounding, cannot be told from a floor in the measure." The README drops the rounding clause without an ellipsis. That clause is the one the next sentence leaves open. It should be quoted in full.

9. **The 0.8 TV does not reproduce Part 119 on the same prefixes, and the README sets this aside.** README line 93 gives 0.645 (real) and 0.583 (control) against Part 119's 0.707 and 0.644, "which Gate 2 does not compare." I reproduce the 119b values from `interaction.jsonl` (0.64507, 0.58328). The Part 119 values come from the README and NUMBERS and I did not recompute them. The same 0.8 prefixes and T were replayed, and the share reproduces to 0.003 and nPair to 0.24%, so a 0.06 TV gap needs an explanation. The TV dose sentence in line 93 ("lower at 0.2 than at 0.8") inherits it.

10. **The real-versus-control contrast reverses at 0.4, and the lead omits the contrasts that bear on it.** Path 2 share at 0.4 is 0.323 for controls and 0.302 for real (README line 89; reproduced). The matched residual at 0.4 favours real pairs (+0.068, README line 150). Triage at 0.4 is 33/741 (4.5%) for real pairs against 20/1,482 (1.3%) for A+R controls. At 0.8 it is 357/741 (48.2%) against 659/1,482 (44.5%; p = 0.10). The lead mentions only real and random pairs. Both contrasts should go in the lead, and the sign difference between raw share and the matched residual should be explained.

11. **119b.9 "held" hides its size, and its coverage mixes denominators.** README lines 144–158. The mean residual 0.0677 is a ratio of about 1.07, with a 95% interval of 0.93 to 1.22 against a bound of 1.25. "Held" means the excess is bounded. It does not mean real pairs interact no more than random directions. The coverage 17,503/19,532 divides by all 0.4 positions, but the effect-matched records use only common-mask positions (scorer line 1107 passes `common_support`). On the same basis the figure is 17,503/19,241 = 91.0%. The verdict is unchanged. The ratio interval and one denominator should be given.

12. **Path 1 omits the 0.8 control rate, and it does not mention a token mismatch.** README lines 192–201. I reproduce 0.48756 and 0.43606 over 727 rows and 4,538 contested positions from `path1.jsonl`, using the scorer's `g` field. Part 119's own `score119.json` gives controls 0.2802 at 0.8 against 0.244 for real pairs (`logit_summary`). The README gives only 0.244, and there are no Path 1 controls at 0.4. The generated token `g` also differs from the both-push top token at 99 of 4,538 contested positions (2.2%), which the README does not mention.

13. **The figure caption and figure do not match.** README line 11 says "Left", "Middle", "Right." The figure stacks three panels vertically: triage and reader on top, interaction share in the middle, slopes at the bottom. The middle panel title conflicts with the 0.8 cell (item 4). The top panel plots pair doses (one push each) and single doses on one α axis, so a two-push pair at 0.8 sits beside one-push singles with no note. The 0.75–1.25 band for 119b.6 is not drawn, though the 0.15 line is.

14. **Several predictions are read at their margin, and the kind-gap wording is wrong.** (a) 119b.6 is held at 0.4 by 0.04: b_A 0.787 and b_2 0.779 against a 0.75 floor. At 0.6 both are below (0.734, 0.749), and at 0.8 they are 0.649 and 0.667 (README lines 88–91). The README reports only 0.4. (b) README line 172 says "the 0.4 median gap was not at most half the 0.8 value." The registered quantity is the median absolute gap (0.2577 against 0.3305; half is 0.1652). (c) The 168 pairs come from 21 slot and 8 concept units (I reproduced 122 positive and both medians from the tournament groups). The README never gives these counts, and the design notes that eight concept units give power only for a large effect.

15. **Terms a stranger cannot follow.** "Broken" is "Part 86's fixed answer-text rule" (line 42), with no description. The design says the rule marks repeats and answers under four words, and keeps answers naming Qwen, Tongyi or Alibaba or carrying an AI word in the first sentence. Also undefined: winner-take-all (margin rule; tie counts as a miss), unit bootstrap, product versus sum weighting, dose, push, persona, neuron (a steering direction, not a single neuron), random direction, A+R, Part 86, 92, 92b, 118 and 119c, Haiku and Luna 6 as readers, A770, bf16 and greedy. Line 38 says b_A and b_2 are slopes "from each single-push change," but the design's method is one no-intercept regression on both single changes. "Part 119c plans a float32 follow-up" (line 5) appears in the README and PREDICTIONS.md, not in the design.

The README records only the pilot's 36 answers (lines 213–217). `/home/elisepc/green/worktrees/ab-part67/part119b/BUILD-NOTES.md` line 11 says the by-eye stopping rule "were not run in this worker environment" and that all eleven predictions are "not read." That contradicts the pilot note and is stale.
