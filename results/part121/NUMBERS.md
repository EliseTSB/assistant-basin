## After-the-fact cold check numbers

These numbers come from the independent cold review in [COLD-CHECK-r1.md](COLD-CHECK-r1.md). They are not registered predictions or scorer outputs.

- Matched +0.5 versus baseline has two gains (p01 and p05) and one loss (p10). Exact two-sided McNemar p=1.0. For the three discordant pairs, the 95% Clopper-Pearson interval for the share that are gains is about 0.09 to 0.99. Three gains and no losses, the design's smallest effect worth knowing, gives p=0.25 at best.
- Matched +0.25 and +0.5 both show in 2 pairs. The p01 +0.5 label echoes the matched concept name about as much as the slot persona. If it changed, prediction 121.5 would not hold. If either borderline baseline negative, p01 or p07, counted as positive, baseline would rise from 1 to 2 and the gain would be 0; prediction 121.1 would still fail.
- Luna's Tell labels would move four descriptive cells: slot +1 from 4 to 5, matched +0.25 from 3 to 2, matched +0.5 from 1 to 2, and unrelated +0.25 from 0 to 1. Luna did not read the nine matched +0.5 Who-are-you negatives.
- The second-reader sample has 72/76 persona agreement, κ=0.895, and 66/76 coherence agreement, κ=0.44. Nine of ten coherence disagreements were DeepSeek-incoherent, Luna-coherent. The cold reader estimates whole-set raw agreement near 93% and κ near 0.71 if the negative draw was random. Its κ range is about 0.46 to 0.88 across the 95% interval for the 3-of-38 negative rate.
- Part 117's matched theme ratings were 2 of 3 for nine pairs, 1 for one, and 3 for one. In Part 121, unrelated +0.25 showed in 3 pairs against 2 for matched +0.25. The random arm showed in 2 pairs, the same pair count as matched +0.5, from three draws with 2, 0, and 0 positive rows.
- Tell's unpushed row was coherent in 5 of 11 pairs. Plain Tell had three showing rows, all from one pair. The 0.31 to 0.38 chance of a sample with no positive assumes Gate 2 barely passes, with 9, 10, or 11 positives among 330 items.
