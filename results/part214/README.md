# Part 214: Qwen3.5-4B's single two-phase partial-zero dial gives 26 clean Google answers of 192 against factual resampling's 28, and reproduces the fact-vs-"you" gap; 4 of 8 predictions held

![Part 214: (a) clean Google answers out of 192 for each Stage 2 arm, with scale_dial_fact beside trace_resample_fact and scale_dial_you beside trace_resample_you, unchanged and zero marked as references; (b) the Stage 1 dial curve of mean delta g_ali against the online scalar alpha, with the scale_dial_fact point and the ten matched random dial sets](fig214.png)

Part 214 asked why factual donor values bring back Qwen3.5-4B's Google fallback when "you" donor values do not, and tested one shared two-phase partial-zero dial against phase, spike and register accounts. The Stage 2 result is that Vesper's H4, one shared two-phase partial-zero dial across the four units, matches factual resampling's Google rate and its fact-vs-"you" gap within the registered 0.05: `scale_dial_fact` gives 26 clean Google answers of 192 against `trace_resample_fact`'s 28, a difference of 0.0104, and the dial gap is 16/192 against the donors' 12/192, a gap error of 0.0208. The decode-phase-content rival does not carry the gap: swapping only the decode phase from factual to "you" values moves a third of it (0.3333), and 214.5 failed. Spike loss is not needed (214.6 held): pooling "you" into a flat mean gives 12 answers against the "you" trace's 16, not a large upward jump. Four predictions held (214.1, 214.3, 214.4, 214.6) and four failed (214.2, 214.5, 214.7, 214.8). The scorer's headline reading is `mixed / inconclusive`, because the design makes the "accounts for" headline conditional on 214.2, a Stage 0 calibration premise measured before any Stage 2 answer existed, and it missed: the two banks' decode-phase projections differ by 0.0752 against a bar of 0.08. The design and all eight predictions are by Gemini 4 Neon (Vesper). Scope: Qwen3.5-4B at one snapshot, Part 212's four-unit set (`L6 N753`, `L7 N1169`, `L11 N8868`, `L29 N776`), Part 66's 24 English held-out phrasings, eager attention in bf16 on the Arc A770, and sampled generation at temperature 0.7 for 48 tokens.

## Does the dial reproduce factual resampling's Google rate and the fact-vs-"you" gap?

Yes, on every registered clause, so 214.4 held. The factual dial gives 26 clean Google answers of 192 against the factual trace's 28, an absolute difference of 0.0104 where the bar was at most 0.05, and it sits 0.1146 above the plain model where the bar was +0.04. Its one broken answer is far below the 28-of-192 ceiling. On the "you" side the dial gives 10 answers against the trace's 16: the dial gap is 0.0833 and the donor gap is 0.0625, so the dial captures 1.3333 of the donor gap where the bar was at least 0.6. The two-sided gap error is 0.0208, inside 0.05; the "you" dial is 0.0313 below the "you" trace, inside 0.05; and the worst-case recode holds, 10 clean plus 0 broken below the factual dial's 26.

## Does swapping only the decode phase carry the gap?

No, and 214.5 failed. `prompt_fact_gen_you`, which swaps only the decode phase from factual to "you" values, gives 24 clean Google answers of 192 against `trace_resample_fact`'s 28. The drop is 0.0208, a share of 0.3333 of the 0.0625 donor gap; the registered bars were more than half (0.03125) and at least 0.03, and both missed. Its worst-case recode held, 24 below 28. The phase triangle is order-dependent and by itself does not separate decode-phase content from prompt-phase carry-over; the design's H2 rival bar is met at 0.0417 against 0.03125, but the scorer does not credit a rival because 214.4 held. The bootstrap interval of `prompt_fact_gen_you` minus `trace_resample_fact` is -0.0208, from -0.078125 to 0.026042, which includes 0. Source: `predictions.214.5`, `stage2.bootstrap` and `headlines.rivals.h2` in [score.json](stage2/score.json).

## Is early-token spike loss needed?

No, and 214.6 held. Pooling the "you" bank into a flat mean (`mean_resample_you`) gives 12 clean Google answers of 192 against the "you" trace's 16, a jump of -0.0208 where the prediction allowed up to +0.05; and zero exceeds the flat mean by 0.1458, above the +0.04 bar. So mean pooling does not reproduce a large spike-loss jump, and nothing here requires early-token spikes. The prediction's own text warns that averaging changes prefill values, decode timing, donor identity and covariance at once, so this reads as a statement about mean pooling, not as evidence that spikes are the mechanism. The bootstrap interval of mean minus trace is -0.0208, from -0.078125 to 0.026042; of zero minus mean it is 0.1458, from 0.057292 to 0.244792. Source: `predictions.214.6` and `stage2.bootstrap` in [score.json](stage2/score.json).

## Does the downstream L27 writer track the dial, and does the three-unit engine hold?

The three-unit engine holds and the downstream tracking does not, so 214.8 failed. Omitting `L29 N776` changes `nat_trace_fact` by 0.0080 and `nat_trace_you` by 0.0130, both inside the 0.25 tolerance, so part (a) held. The correlation over the pooled 16 arms does not: Spearman between the mean `L27 N3325` shift and mean delta g_ali is -0.271 against a bar of at most -0.80. The registered statistic is the pooled 16-arm Spearman. Post hoc and descriptive (COLD-CHECK finding 2), the pooled -0.271 is a block effect: the dial arms alone give -1.000, while the six natural-decode arms' L27 shift is nearly constant at 3.449 to 3.512 and their delta g_ali spans 0.958 to 2.173, a within-block correlation of +0.886. Pooling the two blocks gives -0.271. In this split L27 follows the dial and not the natural donors; the prediction assumed one relation across both blocks. Source: `predictions.214.8` in [score.json](stage2/score.json) and [COLD-CHECK.md](cold-check/COLD-CHECK.md) finding 2.

## Does a polite request without "you" act like "you"?

Not as registered, and the first clause is defective: the polite-request arm moves the way the wording says, and the prediction fails on the clause's inverted sign and a 0.030 miss on clause 2. The three Stage 1 natural-gen means are `nat_trace_fact` 0.972, `nat_trace_imp` 2.173 and `nat_trace_you` 1.842. As registered, clause 1 asks for `nat_trace_fact` minus `nat_trace_imp` at least +0.20 and gets -1.200; clause 2 asks for the absolute difference between `nat_trace_imp` and `nat_trace_you` at most 0.30 and gets 0.330, missing by 0.030 (0.0305 unrounded). Both clauses failed.

COLD-CHECK finding 1 shows that clause 1's sign is inverted relative to its own wording. `delta_g_ali` is measured against `unchanged`. With factual at 0.972 below "you" at 1.842, "shifts away from `nat_trace_fact` toward `nat_trace_you`" requires the imp arm above fact, but the clause requires fact minus imp to be at least +0.20. The data move the way the wording says: imp is 1.200 above fact and 0.330 above "you". On `delta_g_seq` the ordering reverses (fact 5.897, imp 4.937, you 4.971; the absolute imp-to-you difference is 0.034), so the clause reads as drafted against `g_seq` while naming `g_ali`. It fails as registered either way, because clause 2 misses by 0.031. This is a defect in the prediction, reported as such. `D_imp` changes register, words and modifiers at matched length, so no clause here identifies the pronoun. Source: `predictions.214.7` in [score.json](stage2/score.json) and [COLD-CHECK.md](cold-check/COLD-CHECK.md) finding 1.

## What was the Stage 0 premise?

214.2 is a calibration premise, not a post-registration result: the greedy donor recordings were made before any Stage 2 answer existed, and the alphas were pasted into the design before registration with no threshold changed. It failed. The decode-phase projection difference is 0.0752 against a bar of +0.08; the second clause held, with `alpha_gen(fact)` at -0.0014 inside the 0.35 near-zero regime. The collapse branch did not trigger, so `scale_dial_you` ran at (0.4120, 0.0739) rather than the fixed midpoint rung. The two pinned dials are `scale_dial_fact` (0.2474, -0.0014) and `scale_dial_you` (0.4120, 0.0739). Because the design's "accounts for" headline requires 214.2, its failure is why the scorer's headline reading is `mixed / inconclusive` even though 214.4 held on every clause. Source: `predictions.214.2`, `pinning.facts` and `pinning.you` in [score.json](stage2/score.json); the pinned pairs are repeated in its `paste_block`.

## Method

The design is [PART214-DESIGN.md](../../PART214-DESIGN.md) v5, registered 2026-11-20 14:11 CT. The model is Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B at revision `851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a`, loaded in `torch.bfloat16` on the Intel Arc A770 (`xpu`) with `attn_implementation="eager"` as [run.json](stage2/run.json) records, and with thinking off and no system prompt (design lines 84-86). The four units are `L6 N753`, `L7 N1169`, `L11 N8868` and `L29 N776`, intervened on the `down_proj` input, with `L24 N3949` and `L27 N3325` recorded downstream.

Stage 1 runs 60 arms on 25 prompts, for 4,624 batch-1 forwards (24 pool, 100 recordings, and 60 arms × 25 prompts × 3 name histories; Amendment 1). The dial curve, the two dials, the unit and frozen diagnostics and the 20 `zero`/`scale_dial_fact` random controls read exact logits at the fixed maker opening `"I was developed by **"`; the 20 `nat_*` and `random_k:nat_trace_fact` arms open on their donor bank's first five recorded greedy tokens, and the 3 `rep_*` arms on `'The main answer is **'`; the design never mixes the groups. `g_ali` is the Google logit minus the logsumexp of the Alibaba and Tongyi first tokens, and every arm's delta is paired against `unchanged`. The 10-point dial curve applies one alpha to the units' live activations at every scored position; `scale_dial_fact` and `scale_dial_you` apply their prompt alpha on the prompt positions and their decode alpha on the opening steps. Stage 2 generates 1,584 batch-1 answers, 192 per main arm over 24 held-out questions, by sampling with temperature 0.7, top p 0.95 and 48 tokens. `trace_*` arms replace the units' live activations with the donor bank's recorded activations (the donor prompt at prefill, its generation at decode). `broken` is Part 212's screen: empty, fewer than four words, a U+FFFD or `!!!!`, or any three-word n-gram repeated four or more times. Every row is batch size 1; there are no duplicate keys, empty rows or non-finite values; and every answer is classified broken-first, then by the earliest-starting Part 66 maker match, so `clean_google` is the first-named maker. Every interval on this page is a 10,000-draw question-clustered bootstrap with seed 214.

The gates passed: Gate 0 prompt parity, Gate 3b donor length, Gate 1 do-nothing hook (`self_patch` minus `unchanged` 0.0007), Gate 2 positive control, and Gate 3 completeness and baseline sanity. The within-run positive control, `zero` on `"Who created you?"`, gives 4 clean Google answers of 24, and `unchanged` answers all 24 fluency guards correctly. Sources: `stage1.completeness`, `stage1.forward_batch_histogram`, `stage2.n_rows`, `stage2.batch_size_histogram`, `stage2.bootstrap`, `stage2.guard_accuracy`, `stage2.positive_control` and `gates` in [score.json](stage2/score.json); sampling parameters from the design's Stage 2 section and the load from [run.json](stage2/run.json).

## Limits

- The unchanged baseline is thin: 4 clean Google answers come from 2 of 24 questions (q16 3/8, q20 1/8). Zero beats unchanged on 12 questions and loses on 0, so 214.1's direction is broad, but every Stage 2 comparison is anchored on those 4 answers (COLD-CHECK finding 3).
- The metric is the first-named maker. Six clean-Google answers also name Qwen or Alibaba later, and two contradict themselves; an any-Google count changes only `scale_dial_you` (11 against 10) and no verdict (finding 4).
- Stage 1 means include the positive control q24 (`"Who created you?"`), where the natural arms' delta g_ali is negative and the dial arms' positive. Excluding it changes no verdict (214.7 becomes -1.245 / 0.349) (finding 5).
- Answers are truncated prefixes: 1,426 of 1,584 hit `max_new_tokens` (median 34 words), so the classifier reads only the opening attribution (finding 6).
- Descriptive, read by no prediction (design control #6): answers opening "I am (**)Qwen" number 172 of 192 for `unchanged`, 120 for `zero`, 133 and 153 for the factual and "you" traces, 141 and 157 for the factual and "you" dials, 150 for `prompt_fact_gen_you` and 159 for `mean_resample_you`. Among answers that do not open that way, clean Google is 4 of 20 for `unchanged` and 40 of 72 for `zero` (`stage2.openings` in [score.json](stage2/score.json)). The first scoring reported 0 such openings on every arm, because its regex ended in `\b`, which does not match between "Qwen" and "3.5"; the fix (commit c502522f, with a test) changed only this block.
