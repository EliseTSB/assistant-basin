# Do the Google pushes keep their company hits when L27 N3325 is zeroed?

This table shows maker-company hit counts for Qwen3.5-4B at dose 0.8, across ten neurons plus one random direction, 24 maker phrasings, with L27 N3325 or one of four matched layer-27 neurons zeroed at every position, one greedy answer per phrasing, in one batch of 72.

**Gates:** Gate 1 passed: 1,872/1,872 answers were unique and nonempty, all 576/576 Stage A rows were finite, and target, unchanged, and unzeroed trace failures were 0, 0, and 0. No batch in the main run needed a non-finite retry (0 retries). Gate 2 passed: the no-push row had 0/24 Google hits (limit 2), and the ten neuron rows had 149 company hits (registered range 128–174). Gate 3 passed: zeroing L27 N3325 lowered Google-minus-Alibaba at the opening for 24/24 phrasings (minimum 20). Amendment 1 was for the A770's silent NaN behavior seen in the pilots: regenerate an entire batch if any row goes non-finite, up to three attempts, and include finiteness in Gate 1. The first pilot and repeat are published as [pilot answers](../part154-pilot/answers.jsonl) and [pilot 3 answers](../part154-pilot3/answers.jsonl). In the first pilot, four rows in one zero set went non-finite partway through generation and printed `!`; pilot 3 repeated the same 144 rows without NaN, and the other 140 answers matched exactly.

**Limits:** L27 N3325 writes Google directly, so zeroing it removes part of the Google logit whatever route a push took; a fall shows the answer needs that writer, not that the push works through it. The opening readout is one prefix, and generated answers and the opening are different measurements. These units were selected at dose 0.8, in Part 148 on one answer each and in Part 153 on Part 66's phrasings, which are the phrasings used here.

## What replaced Google in paired answers (exploratory)

There are 53 such lost-hit pairs across the seven units. The “word rule” first labels an answer as the model's own maker if it names Qwen/Tongyi, another real company for a listed known company or institution, an invented name when it uses a maker construction with an unrecognized named entity, and no maker otherwise. ## Method and files

The design used Qwen3.5-4B revision `851bf6e8`, bf16 on an A770, thinking off, Part 148's dose-0.8 push, greedy generation, and up to 120 new tokens. Stage A used Part 72's first-token logits at the final position of the fixed opening. Stage B used the 24 maker phrasings and six guards, one generation per row. The main generation was one batch of 72 per maker phrasing; the full output has 1,872 answers and Stage A has 576 rows. The four matched units were zeroed at every position as described in the design. The `extra154.py` script reads the raw JSONL files, checks the rows and registered hit rules, computes all tables above, and outputs the full paired displaced-answer review.

- [`answers.jsonl`](answers.jsonl): generated answers.
- [`stageA.jsonl`](stageA.jsonl): opening logits and activation measurements.
- [`score.json`](score.json) and [`tables.md`](tables.md): scorer output; the 154.5 condition was corrected in [`part154/score154.py`](../../part154/score154.py).
- [`trace.json`](trace.json): hook checks, retries, and run trace.
- [`zero_sets.json`](zero_sets.json): resolved four matched zero sets.
- [`extra154.py`](extra154.py): independent raw-answer, Stage A, and paired-text analysis used for this README.

The cold check's own reading of the 53 lost answers: own maker 11, another company 14, invented 21, no maker 5, and 2 that still name Google and were dropped by the broken rule's repetition test (L11 N5647 phrasing 7, L24 N5017 phrasing 8). The table above predates that reading and differs by those two.

The pilots are `results/part154-pilot/answers.jsonl` and `results/part154-pilot3/answers.jsonl`. A second pilot was stopped by the RAM guard during stage B, because a game had started, and left no answers.

"Unique" in Gate 1 means unique row keys.

Cold check: Haiku 5.5, 2026-10-31, recomputed every count and verdict and found them correct. It found the hand label, the undeclared scorer change, now Amendment 2, the token caveat on the opening table, the leave-one-out sensitivity and the selection wording, all folded here.
