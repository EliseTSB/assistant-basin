You are labelling one hidden activation unit from a language model. The input is a JSON object with "windows" (short text windows where the unit was active) and "marked_tokens" (the token wrapped in [[double brackets]] in each window, in the same order). ⟦PAD×N⟧ marks missing context and is not text.

Return one JSON object and nothing else, with exactly these keys:
{"tally":"most frequent marked tokens or token types with counts","concept":"at most 12 words","fit":"K of N","one_job":1,"kind":"topic","note":"","texts":["...","...","...","..."]}

- tally: tally the most frequent marked tokens or token types with counts, for example "' the' x15, ' a' x2, other x4"; for a context feature say what the windows share. Under 25 words.
- concept: the broadest accurate plain-language description of what the marked tokens have in common, at most 12 words. Add conditions (position, preceding word, sentence role) only if nearly all windows share them: many different past-tense verbs is "past-tense -ed verbs", not "past-tense verbs right after the subject".
- fit: N is the number of windows; K is how many have a marked token that belongs to your concept. A reader who saw only your concept should expect the unit to fire at that marked token.
- one_job: integer 1 to 5 set from K/N alone: 5 = 85% or more; 4 = 65-84%; 3 = 45-64%; 2 = 25-44%; 1 = under 25% or no concept. Common words, punctuation, digits and word fragments are real features; do not lower the score because a feature is plain or common, and do not raise it because it sounds interesting. If the windows split between two unrelated features, count only the larger one.
- kind: exactly one of token, word-family, topic, syntax, format, identity-or-origin (claims about what the speaker is or where it comes from), persona-or-creator (a recurring named voice, character, maker or organisation), other, none.
- note: empty unless something is genuinely unusual.
- texts: last, write 4 new, different short texts (one or two sentences, under 30 words each) that follow the same pattern and would make the unit fire at the same kind of marked token. Vary the topic and wording. Do not copy a window and do not use double brackets.

Example: 21 windows, 15 mark forms of "glimmer", 6 unrelated. {"tally":"glimmer x9, glimmering x4, glimmered x2, other x6","concept":"glimmer word family","fit":"15 of 21","one_job":4,"kind":"word-family","note":"","texts":["The glimmering lanterns lined the harbor.","A glimmer of hope crossed her face.","Stars glimmered above the quiet fields.","He noticed a faint glimmer in the water."]}
The JSON must parse exactly; no markdown fences or prose.
