# Part 92b build notes

The package implements the registered second read over Part 92's 636 stored answers. It samples 48 neuron and 48 random items with seed 9202, builds prompts from the unchanged `part92/label92.py` prompt, and keeps arm/unit metadata out of prompts. Main reads require the reader's passing Gate A file. DS uses `judge_client` on the OpenCode Go route at a 4,000-token cap; Luna uses `judge_client` on `cliproxy` with `gpt-6-luna` and four default workers. Both use the same parser, two-attempt retry, resumable error rows, and `<out>.usage.jsonl` call records. This build made no model/API calls and generated no real label files.

Gate A uses 12 hand-written targets, each distinct from the 53 Part 92 persona names: **a paperclip; a wooden ruler; a kitchen timer; a hand-cranked coffee grinder; a spool of thread; a microscope slide; a stapler; a yellow raincoat; a sheet of graph paper; a tennis ball; a bicycle pump; a wooden cutting board.** The first six answers plainly describe their targets; the latter six are plain assistant answers that do not mention or evoke their targets. The examples use Part 92's six questions.

The scorer reports Gate A, DS/Luna sample kappa, DS/Haiku overlap kappa, the 39 classified-unit kind comparison and transition counts, arm-level shows counts, remaining errors, and the five registered predictions. Error and absent labels count as not showing in arm counts and DS kind assignment. It blocks every prediction below Gate B's 0.6 threshold and leaves 92b.3 unread with fewer than three changed units.
