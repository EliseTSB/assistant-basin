The gate

The gate opens only when the sound is chicken-like (layer 19) and the real-chicken neuron (layer 17) stays quiet: the signature of a person imitating a hen. When it opens it pushes the answer away from "hen" and "rooster". When it is closed the answer is exactly the model's own. Its three numbers are frozen.

Inside the model: the chicken neurons

Each bar is how much louder this neuron fired than ordinary non-chicken sounds (480 ESC-50 clips of 48 kinds). The green tick is where a typical recorded hen lands.

Each bar scores two seconds of your take. The darkest bar is the most chicken moment.

Scored by Google's EmbeddingGemma 2 against 31 reference sounds: real hens, public-domain chickens, Elise's imitations, Claude's synthetic purrs and a few controls. It knows clucking, cackling and egg songs well and purrs not at all. Runs on this PC. Only takes you label are saved, for the gate test. The score is sensitive: re-encoding the same recording can move it by twenty points, so compare takes recorded the same way.
